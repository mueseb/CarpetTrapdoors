**Carpet Trapdoors 2.1.0** – one mod for Fabric, Forge and NeoForge

- New: **copper carpet trapdoors** (Minecraft 1.21+). The recipe accepts every copper trapdoor, waxed or not, at any oxidation stage.
- Closed carpet trapdoors keep the same texture direction as wool, so they blend in no matter how they were placed.
- Fixed: on Forge 1.21 the trapdoors had no crafting recipes and dropped nothing when broken.
- Fixed: the creative tab name on Fabric.
- Carpet trapdoors now use the matching carpet's map colour and sound (the moss one sounds like moss).
- Recipes appear in the recipe book as soon as you pick up the matching carpet.
- Carpet trapdoors count as trapdoors for other mods and game mechanics.
- Item names are translated into German, French, Spanish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Polish, Chinese (simplified) and Japanese; every colour uses Minecraft's own translation.
- Now available for Minecraft 1.18.2, 1.19.2, 1.20.1, 1.21.1, 1.21.11 and 26.3.
